Music

sonicrift

Electronic music by Matthew Finley, released as sonicrift. In the sonic-rift repository, the sounds are written in code, without outside samples.

Pieces from that work include Bionic Body, Space Drift, and Machine Sleep. sonicrift-player is the iPhone app.
